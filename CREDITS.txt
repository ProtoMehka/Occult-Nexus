(https://github.com/mystchonky/Ars-Ocultas), originally licensed under
LGPL-3.0-only. Ars Ocultas dropped Forge support after Minecraft 1.20.1
(NeoForge 1.21.1+ only); Occult Nexus backports the Sacrificial Altar and
familiar system to Forge 1.20.1 under the redistribution/modification
rights granted by the LGPL, and republishes under AGPLv3 as permitted by
the LGPL-3.0/AGPLv3 compatibility clause.

See docs/occult_nexus.md in this workspace for the full porting analysis
and license reasoning.

The "debug_summon_thrallflesh" item texture is reused from Occultism
(https://github.com/klikli-dev/occultism, MIT License), originally
"debug_wand.png".
